Business Rules Document — Batch 2026–28
Owner: Placement Committee · Batch size: 38 students · Review cycle: Weekly and monthly
1. Purpose
The Student Development Program (SDP) aims to build students' professional visibility, practical experience, competitive aptitude, campus involvement, job-relevant skills and professional networks.
This document defines the rules for recording activities, tracking deadlines, calculating compliance and identifying students who require follow-up.

5. Reporting and dashboard rules
The Excel tracker must provide:
Student-wise status: Red, yellow and green status for all six attributes.
Weekly batch overview: Number and percentage of students meeting each weekly requirement.
Monthly batch overview: LinkedIn posts and certification completion against monthly targets.
Overdue register: Student name, attribute, planned end date, days overdue and corrective action.
Initiation register: Activities not initiated across consecutive weekly reviews.
Timeline tracking: Start date, planned end date, actual completion date and deadline revisions.
Evidence register: Links to posts, project records, activity proof, certificates and networking logs.
Follow-up list: Student, unmet requirement, responsible reviewer, action and next review date.
Historical reporting: Previous weekly and monthly statuses must remain available for trend analysis.
The dashboard must distinguish a missing report from a verified lack of progress. A missing report can trigger red for reporting compliance, but the underlying activity status should remain identifiable as unverified until reviewed.
6. Overall student compliance
Overall compliance should be assessed against the requirements applicable to the selected review period.
\[ \text{Compliance \%} = \frac{\text{Students meeting all applicable requirements}} {\text{Students assessed}} \times 100 \]
A student is fully compliant only when all applicable requirements for the selected period are met.
Monthly requirements should not be treated as final failures before the month-end deadline.
Approved exemptions must be documented and handled consistently.
Red statuses should trigger a follow-up action; they should not automatically result in a penalty unless a separate policy has been approved.
7. Implementation decision
There is one important distinction to preserve in the Excel formulas: green for an ongoing, properly scheduled project is not the same as green for a completed project or a monthly certification target.
The tracker should therefore maintain two separate fields:
Timeline status: Green, yellow or red based on initiation, dates and completion.
Target achievement: Met, in progress or not met based on the required output, such as completing one certification during the month.
This prevents an in-progress certification from being counted as a completed monthly certification, while still allowing the student to receive green for following a valid timeline.
HSB Student Development Program — UI Business Rules & Functional Requirements
The system should have two separate interfaces built on a shared database: a Student Portal for submitting and tracking development activities, and a Management Dashboard for monitoring all 38 students, reviewing evidence, and identifying gaps.
One important design rule: the interface should distinguish weekly target achievement from activity timeline compliance. A student may have a project progressing on schedule but still miss a separate weekly target. The system must track both without incorrectly marking the student fully compliant.

Submit updates · Track personal targets · Manage activity timelines · Upload evidence

Management Dashboard
Batch overview · Student-level drill-down · Compliance monitoring · Follow-ups
Authorized access
Both interfaces must use the same underlying student records. Students can update their own information, while authorized management users can review records and monitor batch performance.

BR-UI-04: Saving, editing and submission
Students may save drafts and return to them later.
A saved record must retain its creation date, last-updated date and status history.
Students may edit their own records, subject to the period's submission rules.
Changes to dates after a task becomes overdue must be logged and must not silently remove the original deadline.
A student must not edit another student's records.
Management corrections must record the reviewer, date and reason.
Once a weekly period closes, student editing should be restricted to an approved correction workflow.
Evidence links must be validated for format, and uploaded files must be stored securely if file upload is supported.

Approve justified deadline changes.

View the original and revised values in the audit history.
Only authorized management users may change a verified record's status directly. Every manual override must include a reason, reviewer identity and timestamp.
BR-UI-12: Search, filters and exports
The management dashboard must support:
Search by student name or student ID.
Filter by week, month, year, attribute, mentor and compliance status.
Sort students by overdue duration, missing updates and overall status.
Export the current filtered results to Excel.
Generate an individual student report and a batch summary.
Preserve historical data for comparisons across review periods.
4. Shared system rules
The following rules apply to both interfaces:
Every student has one unique record and can access only their own portal.
Management users receive role-based permissions.
All six attributes must use the agreed business rules for status calculations.
Weekly and monthly reporting periods must be consistent throughout the system.
Dates and deadline changes must be stored in the database, not only displayed in the UI.
Historical submissions must be retained so that earlier reports remain accurate.
Missing information must be identifiable separately from verified non-compliance.
The interface must provide clear validation messages when required fields or evidence are missing.
All dashboard totals must be calculated from the same underlying records to avoid inconsistencies between pages.
The system must work on desktop and mobile screens.

6. Acceptance criteria
The UI is ready for use when the following tests pass:
A student can select a week, open each of the six attributes and submit a valid update.
A project initiated without an end date becomes yellow.
A project past its end date without verified completion becomes red.
A project not initiated for two consecutive weekly reviews becomes red, without incorrectly penalizing a future scheduled start.
A student with one LinkedIn post in a completed month is yellow; two or more is green.
A student with one qualifying networking conversation in a completed week is yellow; two or more is green.
An in-progress certification can be green on its timeline while remaining incomplete against the monthly certification target.
The management dashboard accurately identifies fully compliant, partially compliant and zero-target-achievement students.
Every partial or non-compliant student can be traced to specific unmet requirements.
Management can view each attribute's timeline, evidence and status history.
Changing a deadline does not erase the original deadline or audit trail.
The total number of students in all classifications reconciles with the number assessed, while awaiting-assessment students are reported separately.
Final design principle: the Student Portal should make updating progress simple and transparent; the Management Dashboard should make exceptions, overdue tasks and batch-wide gaps immediately visible. The same business rules must govern both interfaces, so the student and management views never disagree about a record's status.
