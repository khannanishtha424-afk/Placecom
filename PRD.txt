1. Executive Summary
The HSB Student Development Program (SDP) application will help students record and manage professional-development activities while allowing the Placement Committee to monitor progress across the cohort. The application will provide two role-based interfaces backed by one database and one shared status-calculation engine.
Student Portal: choose a reporting week, open one of six attributes, submit progress and evidence, and review current and historical status.
Management Dashboard: review batch-level compliance, identify students requiring follow-up, inspect individual records and timelines, review evidence, approve justified corrections, and export reports.
Core design principle: timeline health and target achievement are separate dimensions. A project can be on schedule while a separate target remains unmet.
All status values and dashboard totals must be calculated from saved records and agreed rules; illustrative sample values must never appear as production metrics.
2. Problem Statement
Student development information is difficult to monitor consistently when updates, deadlines, evidence and follow-ups are maintained separately. The Placement Committee needs a reliable way to see who is progressing, which requirements are incomplete, whether timelines are overdue, and what action is needed. Students need a clear, low-friction process for maintaining accurate records without losing prior submissions.

0 = Red; 1 = Yellow; 2+ = Green. A connection request, follow/following, or simple greeting does not qualify. Count distinct conversations once.
7. Core Business Rules
7.1 Reporting calendar
Weekly review periods follow Monday–Sunday. The first and last periods displayed within a month may be partial calendar weeks.
Each week card displays week number, date range, month and year. The current week is highlighted; future weeks are read-only until they begin.
Historical periods display saved records and status. Students cannot switch periods to erase or bypass overdue submissions.
Monthly targets are evaluated against the selected calendar month and remain provisional until that month closes.
7.2 Timeline and initiation rules
Every record has a unique ID, student ID, attribute type, created timestamp and last-updated timestamp.
For projects, activities and certifications, planned start date, planned end date, actual completion date and status-change date are stored separately.
A future scheduled start is not a failure. Red for non-initiation applies only after two consecutive weekly reviews assess the item as not initiated and the scheduled start is no longer in the future.
An item that has started but has no planned end date is Yellow until a valid end date is recorded.
An incomplete item becomes Red after its planned end date. Passing the end date must never auto-mark an item complete.
A completion claim requires appropriate evidence before it can be accepted.
A deadline revision requires authorized approval, a reason, approver identity and timestamp. The original deadline and prior status history remain visible.
A red status should trigger a follow-up workflow, not an automatic penalty.
7.3 Separate status dimensions
The system must store and calculate (a) timeline status, (b) target achievement and (c) overall student classification separately. For example, an in-progress certification can be Green on its timeline but still not satisfy the monthly completed-certification target.
Dimension
Meaning
Timeline status
Green / Yellow / Red based on initiation, dates, completion and evidence.
Target achievement
Met / In progress / Not met, based on output targets such as post or conversation counts and any adopted monthly certification target.
Overall classification
Fully compliant / Partially compliant / No targets met / Awaiting assessment for the selected reporting period.
7.4 Overall classification
Classification
Rule
Fully compliant
All applicable targets and required reporting rules are met.
Partially compliant
At least one applicable target is met, but at least one other target is not met or remains incomplete.
No targets met
None of the applicable targets has been met.
Awaiting assessment
There is insufficient data to determine compliance reliably. This must remain separate from verified non-compliance.
Monthly target outcomes must be marked provisional before month-end. Approved exemptions must be recorded and applied consistently. Dashboard totals must reconcile: assessed classifications must sum to the assessed population, while Awaiting Assessment is reported separately.
8. Functional Requirements
FR-01 — Authentication and role verification: The application must identify the signed-in user and enforce Student or Management permissions on both UI routes and API endpoints.
FR-02 — Student landing page: Display reporting weeks for the current month, with week number, date range, month/year and current/upcoming/completed state.
FR-03 — Six attribute cards: After a student selects a week, show all six attributes with applicable target, current status, progress count where relevant, short status reason and an action to add/view records.
FR-04 — Attribute-specific entry forms: Provide separate forms and field validation for LinkedIn, Projects, Competitions, Activities, Certifications and Networking.
FR-05 — Draft and save: Allow students to save drafts and return later. Save creation/update timestamps and maintain a history of submitted changes.
FR-06 — Evidence and completion validation: Validate evidence URL format; require completion evidence before accepting completed project/activity/certification records. If uploads are supported, store files securely.
FR-07 — Student history: Allow students to review previous periods, submissions, status explanations and correction requests.
FR-08 — Management overview: Show batch totals for fully compliant, partially compliant, no targets met, assessed and awaiting assessment; show compliance percentage and attribute-wise status distribution.
FR-09 — Follow-up lists: Provide clickable lists for partial/non-compliance and overdue/missing updates. Each row includes student, ID, classification, unmet attribute, reason, deadline/target, corrective action, reviewer and next follow-up date where assigned.
FR-10 — Student detail profile: Show all six attribute statuses, records, evidence, weekly/monthly target achievement, timelines, historical status changes, reviewer comments and follow-up resolution.
FR-11 — Timeline visualization: Provide a timeline/Gantt-style view for projects, activities and certifications, including planned start/end, actual completion, overdue duration and original/revised deadlines.
FR-12 — Attribute-wise batch analysis: For a selected attribute, show counts by status, student names, underlying records, evidence, historical trends and exportable results.
FR-13 — Review and correction workflow: Management can request clarification/correction, record reviewer comments, approve justified deadline changes and assign follow-up actions. Manual status overrides require reason, reviewer and timestamp.
FR-14 — Search, filters and sorting: Search by student name/ID; filter by week, month, year, attribute, mentor and classification/status; sort by overdue duration, missing updates and overall status.
FR-15 — Exports: Export the current filtered results to Excel and generate individual student reports and batch summaries.
FR-16 — Audit trail: Preserve prior values for deadline changes, manual overrides, reviewer decisions and corrections. Record actor, timestamp, reason and before/after values.
FR-17 — Responsive layout: Core student and management workflows must be usable on desktop and mobile.
FR-18 — Shared calculation engine: All views and reports must call the same backend rules so displayed statuses and totals cannot disagree.
9. Key User Journeys
9.1 Student update journey
Student logs in and lands on the current month’s week selector.
Student chooses an available week and sees six attribute cards.
Student opens an attribute, adds or updates the relevant record, and supplies evidence/notes where required.
The system validates fields, saves the record and recalculates status from the shared rules engine.
Student sees saved progress, status explanation and any missing field/evidence requirement.
After a period closes, changes go through the approved correction process rather than overwriting history.
9.2 Management review journey
Management logs in and sees the batch overview for the selected week/month/year.
Management filters the batch by classification, attribute, mentor or missing/overdue status.
Management opens a student profile to inspect records, evidence, timeline and history.
Management requests a correction, records a follow-up, or approves a justified deadline revision.
The system records the action in the audit trail and refreshes affected status calculations and reports.
Management exports the filtered list or a batch/individual report when needed.

11. Non-Functional Requirements
Area
Requirement
Security
Enforce role-based access on backend endpoints; students can only read/write their own records. Do not rely on hiding UI elements as the security boundary.
Data integrity
Validate dates and required fields server-side. Use database constraints and transactions for critical writes.
Auditability
Never silently overwrite original deadlines or closed-period records; retain actor and timestamp for management changes.
Consistency
Calculate statuses and dashboard aggregates server-side from one source of truth.
Usability
Use clear labels, plain-language reasons, consistent Red/Yellow/Green semantics and helpful validation messages.
Responsiveness
Support desktop and mobile viewport sizes.
Performance
For the initial 38-student cohort, dashboard filters and normal page loads should feel responsive; avoid unnecessary repeated database queries.
Maintainability
Keep status rules in a reusable backend service with tests, rather than duplicating logic across screens.
Reliability
Handle missing/invalid inputs gracefully and show errors without losing already saved records.
Privacy
Show each student only their own information. Limit management access to authorized users and avoid exposing evidence links unnecessarily.
12. Acceptance Criteria / Definition of Done
A student can select a valid reporting week and open all six attribute cards.
A student cannot access another student's records by changing a URL, identifier or API request.
LinkedIn status is Red for 0 posts, Yellow for 1 and Green for 2 or more in the selected month; an open month is provisional.
Networking status is Red for 0 qualifying conversations, Yellow for 1 and Green for 2 or more in the selected completed week.
An initiated project/activity/certification without a planned end date is Yellow.
An incomplete timeline item past its current approved end date is Red and is not automatically marked completed.
An item not initiated for two consecutive weekly reviews becomes Red only when the planned start is no longer in the future.
A completed item without required evidence cannot be accepted as completed.
An in-progress certification can have Green timeline status while remaining short of the monthly completion target.
A complete competition update can be Green even when no suitable competition was available; missed competition deadlines are flagged separately.
Changing a deadline retains original deadline, revised deadline, reason, approver and timestamp.
The management dashboard supports all required filters, student drill-down, follow-up lists and Excel exports.
Every red/yellow status displayed to management has an understandable reason linked to a record or missing requirement.
Overall classifications reconcile with assessed counts; Awaiting Assessment is shown separately.
Student and management views show matching statuses for the same student and period.
Historical closed-period records remain available after later edits or deadline revisions.
The application works on desktop and mobile and includes API tests, setup instructions and a Postman collection.
13. Reporting and Metrics
Batch summary by selected period: fully compliant, partially compliant, no targets met, awaiting assessment and assessed counts.
Overall compliance percentage: students meeting all applicable requirements divided by students assessed, multiplied by 100. Awaiting-assessment records are excluded from the assessed denominator and shown separately.
Attribute distribution: red/yellow/green counts and percentages by attribute.
Monthly summary: LinkedIn post counts and certification completions against adopted targets.
Overdue register: student, attribute, record, planned end date, days overdue, status and corrective action.
Initiation register: records not initiated across consecutive weekly reviews.
Follow-up register: student, unmet requirement, reviewer/owner, next review date and resolution status.
Historical trends: comparison of weekly and monthly outcomes without rewriting past reports.
14. Assumptions and Decisions to Confirm
Item
Current treatment / decision needed
Certification monthly target
The source rules say at least one completed certification per month if formally adopted by the Placement Committee. Make this configurable and do not enforce it as a final requirement until approved.
Authentication method
Role-based access is required; the specific authentication provider or local login design is not specified and must be selected during implementation.
Evidence storage
Evidence URLs are supported by the requirements. Secure file upload is optional and should only be implemented if storage and access controls are configured.
Weekly submission cutoff
Weekly periods are Monday–Sunday; exact cutoff time/time zone and the formal correction window after closure need confirmation.
Competition update expectation
The rules define weekly update status but do not specify whether a no-competition update must be explicitly submitted as a separate declaration; implement a clear “no suitable competition this week” option.
Mentor assignments
Dashboard filters include mentor; source roster and mentor assignments must be supplied/imported if this filter is needed.
Manual status overrides
Allowed only for authorized management users, with mandatory reason, reviewer identity, timestamp and audit history.
Approved exemptions
Must be recorded and visible; exact exemption approval policy is outside the supplied rules.

17. Design Principle
The Student Portal should make progress reporting simple and transparent. The Management Dashboard should make exceptions, overdue work and batch-wide gaps immediately visible. Both interfaces must use the same saved data and business-rule engine.
